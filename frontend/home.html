DRDO team and task management platform

Coordinated execution for divisions, groups, activities, and teams on your internal LAN.

Saralta brings your operational hierarchy, task assignment, approval flow, proof-based completion, chat, history, and analytics into one controlled workspace designed for mission-focused internal collaboration.

Platform purpose

What this software does

This application is a structured coordination system for internal DRDO work. It mirrors your reporting chain from division level down to team level, then connects that hierarchy directly to execution through tasks and subtasks.

Built from your project flow

How work moves inside Saralta

  • Admins and heads define the organizational hierarchy: division, group, activity, and team.
  • Authorized users create tasks, distribute responsibility, and track due dates, priority, and ownership.
  • Members collaborate with subtasks, activity chat, extension requests, and proof-based completion.

Operational controls

Why it fits a controlled LAN environment

  • Runs as an internal system for organizational use without depending on public cloud workflow tools.
  • Supports role-based control for admins, division heads, group heads, project directors, team leads, and members.
  • Keeps execution evidence, approval checkpoints, history logs, and workspace analytics inside the same environment.

Hierarchy workflow

How structure flows into execution

Saralta mirrors your operational chain from leadership structure to on-ground task execution. Each stage feeds the next, so ownership and reporting stay clear.

Division Strategic control

Top-level mission area and leadership scope.

Group Functional channel

Breaks the division into focused operating groups.

Activity Work package

Defines the actual programme, buildup, study, or mission stream.

Team Execution unit

Assigns accountable people for coordinated delivery.

Task Tracked output

Turns hierarchy into measurable work, approvals, and closure.

Core capabilities

What your project already supports

  • Hierarchy management for divisions, groups, activities, and teams.
  • Task creation with single or multi-assignee ownership, lead assignment, share percentage, priority, and due dates.
  • Approval-aware execution for task creation, task type control, completion review, and deadline extension requests.
  • Activity-level communication and history tracking for operational transparency.
  • Statistics and hierarchy matrix views for monitoring delivery and organizational coverage.
